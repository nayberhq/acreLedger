Fiscal scope

Our target measure is attributable eligible taxes minus eligible government spending, normalized per municipal land acre. Social Security and Medicare payroll taxes and benefits are excluded. Observed, allocated, and unallocated amounts will be disclosed separately. Government transfers must not be double counted across jurisdictions.

Until tax and expenditure records can be reconciled for a city and fiscal year, its net balance remains unavailable.

Data availability and methodology

The municipality index comes from the 2024 Census Gazetteer. Census-designated places are marked and are not incorporated municipalities. Financial records currently cover only three pilot municipalities (2022 and 2024 where available). Other places show unavailable, not zero. The current financial item codes have not yet been classified into revenue and expenditure totals, so net fiscal balances cannot be calculated.

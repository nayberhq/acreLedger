What does your city contribute?

Compare estimated taxes contributed against public spending received, per acre, per resident, or in total. This first release demonstrates the interface with invented figures, not actual municipal financial results.

Methodology

Net fiscal balance is eligible taxes attributable to a municipality minus eligible government spending received. The intended model excludes Social Security and Medicare payroll taxes and corresponding benefits. Local fiscal flows, cross-government transfers, and shared expenditures must be reconciled before publishing real estimates.

Future releases will show observed and modeled amounts separately, source years, uncertainty ranges, and spending attribution rules. A positive or negative balance is not a judgment of municipal management.

⚠ All financial, demographic and acreage values on this prototype are synthetic and must not be cited as actual results.
